# Brent Crude Oil Analysis

This project was made with the intention to learn how to work with real financial market data in Python and to explore unique ideas around Brent crude prices during the Iran conflict.

## What is included:

- Cleaning and checking more than 1.1 million one-minute Brent observations downloaded from HistData.
- An event study using 14 scheduled events where the US president was expected to give comments on Iran, looking at price movements before and after the events over several time windows.
- A Friday-to-Monday test during the Iran conflict to compare holding a position over the weekend with closing on Friday and reopening on Monday. The reason for this test was to see if the volitivity of the market across the period meant that investors may need to protect themselves from potentially significant developments occurring over the weekend.
- Correlation, regression, directional tests and leave-one-out tests to check statistical significance.

## What I learned:

I learned how to handle a large dataset and how vital cleaning and checking data before using it is, and how to turn an idea about market behavior into something that can actually be tested. This project also taught me just how important statistical analysis is when working with large data sets, whilst it was disappointing to see that my results were not statistically significant I believe this is an incredibly valuable lesson and one I shall carry with me from now on.

AI was used extensively and effectively while writing and debugging parts of the Python. This helped me work through problems that I did not already know how to solve and made me much more comfortable learning unfamiliar methods. Overall The use of AI in this project has been proactive and has allowed me to learn and experience far more than I otherwise would have at this stage in my education.
